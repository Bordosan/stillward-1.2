Stillward Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

The short version: Stillward is free, and you can use every meditation without an account. We don't show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or sell your data. If you choose to sign in to sync, we store your email address and practice data only to keep your progress in sync across your devices.

Who we are

Stillward ("the app") is developed and published by Bordosan LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Using Stillward without an account

You don't need an account to use Stillward. Without one, your settings (such as your chosen voice, default session length, and voice speed) and your practice history are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Optional sign-in to sync

If you choose to create an account to sync your progress across devices, we collect:

  • Your email address, used to create your account and let you sign in. We don't use it for marketing.
  • Your practice data and settings, such as sessions completed, durations, and preferences, so they can be restored on your other devices.

This information is stored on servers operated by us or our hosting provider and is used only to provide the sync feature. You can stop syncing at any time by signing out.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all synced data at any time from the app's settings, or by emailing us at the address below. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your email address and synced practice data from our servers.

Information we do not collect

  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • The app doesn't use your microphone or camera. Meditation audio is played to you; nothing is recorded.
  • We don't use advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Stillward has no purchases or subscriptions, so we never handle payment information.

Meditation audio

Guided meditation audio is included with the app and plays from your device. Listening to a session doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else.

Crash and diagnostic data

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How we protect your information

Synced data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep only what the sync feature needs, and we don't share it with third parties except our hosting provider, which stores it on our behalf.

Children's privacy

Stillward is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. If you have an account, you can delete it at any time as described above, or contact us with any request and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. We will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to Bordosan LLC at bordosanllc@proton.me.
